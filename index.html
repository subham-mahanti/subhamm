Ph.D. candidate at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay working on the development of avalanche photodetectors based on two-dimensional (2D) materials, including MoS₂ and WSe₂. My research involves device fabrication, electrical and optoelectrical characterization, and the implementation of single-photon detection schemes to evaluate key performance metrics. The objective is to assess and benchmark the performance of 2D material-based avalanche photodetectors against commercially available conventional APD technologies, with a strong focus aligned with the National Quantum Mission (NQM).
